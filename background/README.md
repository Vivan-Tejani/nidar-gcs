# Custom Page Background

Place your wallpaper image or video here.

To change the background of the Ground Control Station:
1. Replace `background.jpg` with any image or video you want, OR
2. Drop any image (`.jpg`, `.jpeg`, `.png`, `.webp`, `.avif`) or video (`.mp4`, `.mov`, `.webm`) into this folder.

The system will automatically load and display it across the GCS chassis. Videos will automatically stream, loop, and playback smoothly with hardware acceleration.
